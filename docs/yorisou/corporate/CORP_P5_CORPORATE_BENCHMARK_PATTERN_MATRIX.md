# CORP-P5 — Corporate benchmark pattern matrix

References studied for **mechanism**, not appearance: Palantir, Anthropic, Scale AI, Vercel, and the
broader convention set of technical-company sites (Stripe, Linear).

| # | Mechanism | Seen in | COPY / ADAPT / AVOID | What we actually did |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Company thesis as the first thing, before any product | Palantir, Anthropic | **COPY** | The h1 is the thesis sentence; no product is named above it |
| 2 | Portfolio as *distinct* work, not a uniform card grid | Palantir (platforms), Anthropic (models vs research) | **COPY** | Mirai Move renders as a NETWORK, Kakari as a bounded PROCEDURE |
| 3 | Stated maturity per product | Anthropic (model availability), Vercel (GA/beta) | **COPY** | A stage chip on every project, carried verbatim from its canonical source |
| 4 | Extreme typographic restraint; hairlines, not cards | Palantir, Linear | **COPY** | One accent colour used only as rules, dots and focus; no shadows, no cards |
| 5 | Dense information without visual noise | Palantir, Stripe | **ADAPT** | Numbered rows with a fixed index column; long form behind `<details>` |
| 6 | Progressive disclosure instead of a wall of text | Stripe, Vercel docs | **ADAPT** | Each principle shows a one-line summary; full text is one disclosure away |
| 7 | One structural motif carried across pages | Linear, Vercel | **ADAPT** | The seam: 人 / 仕組み with content crossing only where Yorisou bridges |
| 8 | Motion that expresses state, used sparingly | Linear | **ADAPT** | A single finite 820ms seam draw; fully drawn under reduced-motion |
| 9 | Dark, high-contrast "serious technology" palette | Palantir, Scale AI | **AVOID** | A Japanese paper ground reads as trustworthy locally; enterprise-dark reads as intimidating |
| 10 | Customer logo walls and metric counters | Scale AI, most SaaS | **AVOID** | We have no customers to name. A logo wall would be fabrication |
| 11 | AI-gradient/glow/particle visual language | most AI startups | **AVOID** | Explicitly excluded; zero gradients, glows, particles or blobs |
| 12 | A chatbot as proof of "AI-native" | many | **AVOID** | AI-nativeness is in the information architecture, not a widget; no provider SDK exists in this repo, so a runtime model would be a new dependency and a new secret |
| 13 | Hero product screenshot / device mock | Vercel, Linear | **AVOID** | Both products are pre-GA; a mock would imply a maturity that does not exist |
| 14 | Scroll-jacking / pinned storytelling | many marketing sites | **AVOID** | Breaks keyboard and reduced-motion, and Japanese long-form reading |

**The through-line:** copy the *discipline* of these sites (thesis first, stated maturity, restraint,
differentiated portfolio) and refuse the *props* (logo walls, metrics, gradients, chatbots) that all
require claims YORISOU cannot currently evidence.
