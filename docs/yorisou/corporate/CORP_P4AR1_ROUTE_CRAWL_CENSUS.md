## What CORP-P4A got wrong

Its robots candidate claimed everything personal, authenticated, internal and legacy was disallowed.
With 135 page routes and roughly twenty rules, that was false: about 28 legacy public routes
(`/ai-advisor`, `/business`, `/concept`, `/en`, `/explore`, `/insights`, `/methodology`, `/notice`,
`/partners`, `/pilot`, `/privacy`, `/services`, `/support`, `/terms`, `/vision` and more) were
crawlable. Rules were also written in prefix form only — `/tests/` does not necessarily cover the
exact path `/tests`. Both are fixed: the default is now deny, and exact and descendant forms are
tested independently.
